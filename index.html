Market to target

Audience
English-speaking, high-pay countries: United States ($5.09 per 1,000 views), Canada, UK, Australia and the Nordics.
Niche
Education, finance, business and tech pay the most. Education also shows an ad on 77% of views.
Format
Videos over 8 minutes so mid-roll ads can run. Shorts pay 3–14% as much.
Steadier choice
News and politics varies least between channels, at a lower median of $2.60.
